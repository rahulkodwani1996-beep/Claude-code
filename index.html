Frequently Asked

How do I use Feline Gut Boost and how much should I give?
Sprinkle ½ teaspoon (≈2g) directly onto your cat's wet or dry food once daily — no mixing required. The micro-crumble clings to food naturally. For kittens under 6 months, use ¼ teaspoon. Most cats show improved appetite within 3 days and digestive improvements by day 7.
Are the probiotics actually alive? Do they survive stomach acid?
Yes. Our micro-encapsulation technology wraps each colony in an acid-resistant protein shell. Third-party lab testing confirms >85% CFU viability past feline gastric pH (1.5–2.0). Cultures remain shelf-stable at room temperature for 18 months — no refrigeration needed. This is fundamentally different from loose probiotic powders.
My cat is extremely picky. What if they refuse it?
The 85% organ-meat content produces an intense, meaty aroma that activates a cat's natural hunting-feeding response — even confirmed picky eaters rarely refuse it. We suggest starting with a tiny pinch mixed into a favourite wet food, increasing to a full serving over 3–4 days. If your cat still refuses within 7 days, our guarantee covers a full refund.
How do subscriptions work — can I pause or cancel easily?
Subscriptions auto-renew monthly and can be paused, skipped, or cancelled at any time via your account dashboard or by sending a single WhatsApp message. No lock-in periods, no cancellation fees, no retention calls. We ship 3 days before your billing date to ensure no gap in supply.
Is it safe for kittens, senior cats, or cats on medication?
Safe for cats aged 3 months and above, including seniors. For cats on immunosuppressants, antibiotics, or chemotherapy, consult your vet before introducing any probiotic supplement — as a general precaution, not because of a known interaction.
