Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you order.

How do I use Feline Gut Boost — and how much should I give?
Simply sprinkle ½ teaspoon (≈2g) of Feline Gut Boost over your cat's regular wet or dry food once daily. No mixing or soaking required — the micro-crumble texture clings to food. For kittens under 6 months, use ¼ teaspoon. Results are typically noticeable within 5–7 days of consistent use.
Are the probiotics actually alive, and do they survive stomach acid?
Yes — our micro-encapsulation technology wraps each probiotic colony in an acid-resistant protein shell. Third-party testing confirms over 85% viable CFU delivery past feline gastric pH. Unlike loose probiotic powders, our cultures remain shelf-stable at room temperature for 18 months without refrigeration.
My cat is extremely picky. What if they refuse it?
The high offal content (85% chicken liver, heart, and kidney) produces a naturally pungent, meaty aroma that even the pickiest cats find irresistible. We recommend starting with a tiny pinch mixed into their favourite wet food, then gradually increasing to a full serving over 3–4 days. If your cat still refuses within 7 days, our satisfaction guarantee covers a full refund.
How do subscriptions work — can I pause or cancel easily?
Absolutely. Subscriptions auto-renew monthly and can be paused, modified, or cancelled at any time via your account dashboard or by sending us a WhatsApp message. There are no lock-in periods, no cancellation fees, and no annoying retention calls. We ship 3 days before your billing date so there's no gap in supply.
Is Feline Gut Boost safe for kittens, senior cats, or cats on medication?
Feline Gut Boost is formulated for cats aged 3 months and above. For senior cats, the probiotic and organ-meat combination actively supports nutrient absorption and stool quality. If your cat is on immunosuppressants, antibiotics, or undergoing chemotherapy, please consult your vet before introducing any probiotic supplement — as a general precaution, not because of a known interaction.
